Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

EC3 Enterprises Incorporated respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you interact with our website, customer-support channels, and SMS program.

Information we may receive

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, mobile phone number, and the contents of your inquiry or support request. Our website hosting provider may also process limited technical information needed to operate and secure the site.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide requested customer support, maintain business records, operate and secure our services, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

SMS information and consent

If you opt in to our SMS customer-care program, we use your mobile number and messaging consent only to provide the messaging program you requested, maintain consent and opt-out records, and support related customer-care activities.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

Your choices

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or email info@ec3enterprises.net.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to info@ec3enterprises.net.
